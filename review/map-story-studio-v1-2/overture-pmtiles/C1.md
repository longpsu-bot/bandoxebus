## Publication boundary

C1 uses the official pinned Overture current-release archive for authoring/current-release use. The official archive's 60-day retention means it is not a durable publication mechanism. The C2 durable project-area PMTiles publication snapshot is intentionally deferred; C1 adds no proxy, tile server, service worker, alternate provider, or per-Scene extraction.
